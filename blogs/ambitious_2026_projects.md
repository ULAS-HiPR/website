Our latest all-hands meeting marked the start of the rest of our projects for the year.

Members have been busy building their own L1 rockets, while the liquid-engine development team has already been working for months. The Mach, EuRoC and other project teams are now dividing into their disciplines and beginning the design work.

With more than 90 members, it has been an unprecedented challenge to find enough engineering problems for all of the motivated engineers, designers and software developers who joined us this year. 2026 will be our most ambitious year yet, and this is only the beginning.
