ULAS HiPR has been featured in Enterprise Ireland's *Space Activities in Ireland 2024* report.

The report recognises the team's hands-on innovation and contribution to Ireland's growing student-rocketry ecosystem. It is encouraging to see the progress being made across the Irish space industry, and we are proud to be part of it.
