ULAS HiPR represented Immersive Software Engineering at the Stripe Young Scientist & Technology Exhibition, introducing students to rocketry and the space projects available at the University of Limerick.

Co-Head Dervla Gargan and flight-computer lead Seán O'Sullivan joined the University of Limerick stand to explain how software is integrated into the team's rockets and experimental systems.
