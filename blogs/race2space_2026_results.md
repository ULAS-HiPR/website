Five successful hot fires and the Best Newcomer award: Lúin of Celtchar completed its Race 2 Space 2026 test campaign after nine months of development.

Our student propulsion team tested the 2 kN liquid bipropellant engine on 8 July at Airborne Engineering's facility in Westcott Venture Park. Lúin is Ireland's first additively manufactured liquid rocket engine.

![Lúin of Celtchar firing during the Race 2 Space test campaign](/race2space_2026/01.jpg)

The campaign recorded an average peak specific impulse of **212 s** and **97% combustion efficiency**, with testing reaching **150% of nominal chamber pressure**. The team also received a Most Patriotic Team mention for bringing the Irish flag to the competition.

| ![ULAS HiPR receiving the Best Newcomer award](/race2space_2026/07.jpg) | ![The team presenting Lúin of Celtchar at the Race 2 Space symposium](/race2space_2026/03.jpg) |

With a working engine, we are exploring how Lúin could support future propulsion testing through EirSEDS, alongside other Irish universities and rocketry teams. Developing test infrastructure in Ireland would give students, researchers and industry more opportunities to develop and test propulsion systems at home.

Thank you to **Irish Manufacturing Research**, **FactoryXChange 2.0**, **OMC Engineered Solutions**, the **University of Limerick**, and its **Faculty of Science and Engineering** for supporting Lúin's development.
